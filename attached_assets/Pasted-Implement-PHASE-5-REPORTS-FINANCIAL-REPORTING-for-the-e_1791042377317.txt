==================================================
5M — REPORT DATA INTEGRITY
==================================================

This is critical.

Validate calculations against existing Phase 4 behavior.

Examples:

1. Completed sale:
   Sale contributes revenue + COGS + GST.

2. Partial sale return:
   Only returned quantity/revenue/cost/GST is reversed.

3. Full sale return:
   Original sale becomes fully reversed for financial reporting.

4. Cancelled invoice:
   Revenue, stock and customer due effects are reversed exactly once.

5. Invoice correction:
   Original transaction must not be counted together with the corrected replacement in a way that doubles revenue/profit.

6. Credit sale:
   Sales report includes sale.
   Customer Due report reflects outstanding balance.

7. Customer collection:
   Does not reduce sales revenue.
   It reduces outstanding customer balance only.

8. Purchase:
   Included in purchase totals.

9. Purchase return:
   Reduces net purchase value and appropriate GST/input values.

10. Supplier payment:
   Does not reduce purchase expense/value again.
   It reduces supplier outstanding balance.

11. Historical transaction:
   Current GST settings, current medicine price, or current purchase cost must NOT change historical reports.
